R / Role and story

About me

I'm an Enterprise Data Vault Architect at Colruyt Group India in Hyderabad, with more than 16 years in data and business intelligence. Before this role I worked as a Principal Business Consultant and BI Architect.

Day to day I design Data Vault 2.0 models on Snowflake: hubs, links, satellites, PITs and the infomarts that sit on top, across supply chain, finance and retail.

Outside work I design logos, build apps and experiment with agentic and generative AI. I like the place where creativity meets logic.
